Publish data points to evaluate the alarm.

What to learn

CloudWatch can evaluate an alarm as M breaching datapoints out of the latest N evaluation periods. This reduces noise compared with alarming on one spike.

  • N is the size of the evaluation window.
  • M is the number of breaching datapoints required.
  • Missing-data treatment and metric math can change real alarm behavior.
Example: 2 out of 3 [breach, OK, breach] → ALARM
