Identity and Access Management (IAM) - Advanced

Advanced IAM combines grants with guardrails. Roles grant temporary access; boundaries and SCPs limit the maximum possible access.

✓
THE MAIN IDEAA guardrail does not grant permission by itself. An Allow must still exist.
