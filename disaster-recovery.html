Disaster recovery strategy

Match recovery time and recovery point objectives to an AWS disaster recovery pattern.

Recovery objectives

How quickly the service must return.
How much recent data loss is acceptable.

Recovery pattern

The four common strategies

  1. Backup and restore
  2. Pilot light
  3. Warm standby
  4. Multi-site active/active

As recovery speed increases, ongoing cost and operational complexity usually increase too.

Exam distinction

RTO measures downtime. RPO measures acceptable data loss. The question may give only one, so do not assume they are the same.
