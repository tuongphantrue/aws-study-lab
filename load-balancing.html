What to learn

A load balancer is a traffic distribution and health-isolation layer, not simply a round-robin proxy.

  • Only healthy registered targets receive new requests.
  • Least-outstanding-request routing can favor the target currently doing the least work.
  • Health checks need thresholds and intervals in real deployments, so removal is not instantaneous.
