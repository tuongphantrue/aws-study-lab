What to learn

AWS authorization starts with an implicit deny. A request needs an applicable Allow, while an applicable explicit Deny wins over any Allow.

  • Identity-based and resource-based policies can grant access.
  • Permissions boundaries and organization policies limit the effective permission set.
  • The real AWS evaluator has important exceptions and cross-account rules; this lab models the common mental model.
effective ≈ (identity Allow ∪ resource Allow) ∩ boundary ∩ SCP explicit Deny → DENY
