Know exactly what the SAA-C03 exam is testing

The exam is about choosing secure, resilient, high-performing, and cost-optimized architectures—not remembering every AWS menu or API command.

HOW SCORING WORKS

50 questions are scored; 15 are unscored

Scored questions

Fifty questions affect your final result. AWS reports a scaled score from 100 to 1,000 rather than a simple raw percentage.

Unscored questions

Fifteen questions are used by AWS to evaluate possible future exam questions. They are mixed into the exam and are not identified.

Unanswered questions

An unanswered question is scored as incorrect. There is no penalty for guessing, so choose an answer before time expires.

Compensatory score

You do not need to pass every domain separately. Your overall exam score determines whether you pass.

QUESTION TYPES

Read the instruction before choosing

Multiple choice

One correct answer and three distractors. Choose the single option that best meets every requirement.

Multiple response

Two or more correct answers from five or more options. The question tells you how many to select.

This site does not calculate an AWS scaled score.

Mock results are raw practice percentages. Use them to find weak topics, not to predict an exact AWS score.
