Compute cost optimizer

Choose an AWS compute pricing model from workload stability, flexibility, and interruption tolerance.

Compute usage

Recommended purchase option

Core pricing choices

  • On-Demand: no commitment; best for unknown or temporary use.
  • Compute Savings Plans: flexible commitment across EC2, Fargate, and Lambda.
  • EC2 Instance Savings Plans / Reserved Instances: more configuration-specific.
  • Spot Instances: deep discount for interruption-tolerant workloads.

Architecture first

Pricing discounts do not replace right-sizing, Auto Scaling, storage lifecycle policies, or deleting unused resources.
