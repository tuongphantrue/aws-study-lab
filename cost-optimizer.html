1 · Start here

The big idea

On-Demand buys flexibility, Savings Plans and Reserved Instances reward commitment, and Spot rewards interruption tolerance.

2 · Step by step

What AWS does

  1. Identify the steady baseline and temporary peaks.
  2. Decide whether the workload can commit for one or three years.
  3. Decide whether interruptions are acceptable.
  4. Combine pricing models instead of forcing the whole workload into one option.
3 · When to use it

Choose it for these requirements

  • On-Demand for new or short-lived workloads
  • Savings Plans or RIs for a steady baseline
  • Spot for fault-tolerant batch and flexible workers
4 · Exam tip

Words that guide the choice

Compute Savings Plans are broader than configuration-specific commitments and can cover eligible EC2, Fargate, and Lambda usage.

5 · Common mistake

Do not confuse these ideas

The lowest hourly price is not always the lowest total cost after transfer, retrieval, idle capacity, and operations.

RememberUnknown: On-Demand. Steady: commitment. Interruptible: Spot.
