What to learn

S3 Lifecycle rules automate transitions and expiration based on object age and filters. They do not understand your business value or retrieval requirements.

  • Storage classes differ in access assumptions, retrieval behavior, and minimum-duration rules.
  • Lifecycle transitions can have request costs.
  • Objects smaller than 128 KB have special default transition behavior in current S3 Lifecycle rules.
